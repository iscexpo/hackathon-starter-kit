# Participant FAQ

## Do I need to use one of the included templates?

No. They are optional starting points. Use your preferred stack if the project follows event rules and is reviewable.

## Does my project need to be fully finished?

Follow the event's rules. A small working prototype with honest documentation is usually easier to evaluate than a large unfinished idea, but the official event requirements take precedence.

## Can I use AI tools?

Check the event-specific policy in `EVENT_CONFIG.md` or the official event page. Disclose tools and assistance as required, verify generated code, and make sure the team understands and can explain its submission.

## Can I use external code, images, datasets, or APIs?

Only when the terms permit your use. Document sources, licensing, attribution, service requirements, and relevant limitations.

## What if my live demo fails?

Follow the event's published demo policy. If recordings or screenshots are allowed, prepare them in advance. Explain honestly what was working and what failed.

## Where do I submit?

Use only the official submission channel and deadline published by the organizer. Completing `SUBMISSION.md` by itself does not send a submission.

## Who can help?

Use the help channel listed in the event's official details. The reusable kit does not provide an event-specific contact until organizers fill it in.
